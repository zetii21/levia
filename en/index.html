Frequently asked questions

Does Levia tell me what dose to take?No. Levia is a log: you enter the schedule your doctor gave you and the app reminds you. It never suggests or changes a dose.
Which medications does it work with?Semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide and dulaglutide, plus any other with a schedule you define.
Where is my data stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account, no servers and no ads. If you delete the app, the data is deleted.
What's in the free version?The weekly pillbox, dose reminders, site rotation, weight and the journal. Pro adds the analysis.
How do I contact support?Write to ayuda@ferpel.es and we'll reply as soon as we can.
