Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Levia is an app for keeping a personal log of a treatment. This policy explains, in a few words, what happens to your data: it stays on your iPhone.

What data Levia stores

What you log: medication and schedule, doses and injection sites, weight, height, symptoms, protein and water, your goals and your reminder preferences.

Where it is stored

Only on your device. Levia has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer does not receive and cannot see anything you log. If you have iPhone backup turned on, Apple may include the app's data in that backup depending on your iCloud settings.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect personal or health data.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone.

Purchases

Levia Pro subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Levia only receives confirmation from Apple of whether the purchase is active; it does not see your payment details.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your own iPhone. They do not go through any server.

Export and erase

With Levia Pro you can export your data as PDF or CSV from Settings; the file is shared only with whoever you choose. To erase everything, use "Erase all data" in Settings or delete the app.

Minors

Levia is not intended for anyone under 18.

Medical notice

Levia is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. It does not suggest doses or changes to your treatment.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. For any privacy question, write to ayuda@ferpel.es. Data controller: Pelayo Fernandez Gonzalez.
